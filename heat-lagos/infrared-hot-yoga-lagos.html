30-32°C radiant heat on Praia da Batata. The only infrared hot yoga studio in the Algarve.

What Makes Infrared Hot Yoga Different?

Traditional hot yoga studios blast forced air at 40°C, creating an oppressive environment where you're gasping for breath. Our infrared panels emit radiant heat that warms your body directly — like the sun, but without the UV.

The result? You get all the benefits of hot yoga without the dizziness, nausea, or exhaustion:

  • 30-32°C sweet spot: Hot enough to open muscles, not so hot you can't breathe
  • Radiant heat: Warms your body and the floor, not the air
  • Deep detox: Infrared penetrates tissue and releases toxins through sweat
  • Joint-friendly: The floor stays warm, making stretches accessible and safe
  • Sustained energy: You'll feel energized after class, not depleted

The Science of 30-32°C Infrared Heat

Our infrared panels operate at 30-32°C because this is the optimal temperature for:

  • Muscle pliability without overheating
  • Connective tissue receptivity
  • Cardiovascular work without stress
  • Deep sweating without exhaustion

At 40°C (Bikram-style), your body is fighting to cool itself. At 30-32°C, your body is optimizing itself.

The Class Experience

Our infrared hot yoga classes are Vinyasa-based, flowing sequences that build heat from the inside out — then the infrared panels add the external warmth that makes everything deeper.

Each class includes:

  • Dynamic warm-up with breath-to-movement coordination
  • Standing sequences that build strength and balance
  • Floor work with deep hip openers and twists
  • Core activation integrated throughout
  • Savasana with cool-down and guided relaxation

Who Is This For?

Infrared hot yoga is for everyone, but it's especially beneficial for:

  • Athletes: Deep muscle release and flexibility work
  • Stiff bodies: The heat makes everything accessible
  • Detox seekers: Deep sweating without the shock
  • Stress relief: The combination of heat + movement + breath is unmatched
  • Tourists: Perfect recovery after surfing, hiking, or travel
