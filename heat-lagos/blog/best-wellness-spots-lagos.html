Lagos Wellness

10 Best Wellness Spots in Lagos for Yoga, Pilates and Recovery

An honest guide to every wellness spot in Lagos that matters. Studios, spas, massage, and recovery. No pay-to-play, no sponsorships.

How this list was made

This list covers the full wellness landscape in Lagos, not just yoga and pilates studios. It includes massage, spa, sauna, contrast therapy, and recovery-focused spaces. We visited or verified every entry through direct experience, booking platforms, or local reputation.

Lagos is a surf town. Most people here are active, and wellness means recovery as much as exercise. The best spots understand that balance.

1. Algarve Alchemy - yoga, pilates, sauna and ice bath

Why it ranks first: Alchemy is the only wellness space in Lagos old town that combines movement with contrast therapy in one location. The infrared sauna and ice bath sit above Black and White Cafe on Rua Candido dos Reis, making it easy to book a class and a recovery session in the same visit.

What you get: Yoga, pilates, somatic movement, and contrast journeys (sauna plus ice bath). The space is sunlit and intentionally designed for calm.

Who it is for: People who want wellness as a full experience, not just a class. The contrast therapy makes it ideal for surf recovery, post-run relief, and anyone who trains hard.

Visit Algarve Alchemy

2. Heat Lagos - infrared-heated yoga, pilates and recovery

Why it ranks second: Heat Lagos is the most modern wellness studio in Lagos. The space is purpose-built with plenty of room, modern new equipment, and the only infrared-heated classes in town. Infrared heat penetrates deeper than forced air, warming muscles and joints from the inside. The Heat Recovery class is built specifically for active bodies - surfers, runners, cyclists - who need to release tension without aggressive stretching.

What makes it different: Most yoga studios in Lagos focus on spiritual, traditional styles. Most pilates studios focus on slow reformer work. Heat Lagos offers modern, challenging fitness classes that actually deliver results. Sculpt is the most popular class - a hardcore session you will feel for two days. Heat Yin is very popular for winding down. Heat Flow and Heat Pilates are the mellower options when you want movement without intensity.

What you get: Heat Recovery (gentle mobility in the warmth), Heat Yin (long holds to release fascia), Heat Mobility (targeted joint and muscle work), plus Heat Pilates and Heat Sculpt for strength.

Who it is for: Active people who want recovery that actually works. The infrared heat makes muscles more pliable, which means you get more from every stretch without forcing anything.

Price: Intro Offer 79 EUR for 2 weeks unlimited. Drop-in 22 EUR. Vacation Week 59 EUR for 7 days. Monthly memberships from 125 EUR.

Book a class at Heat Lagos

3. Cascade Spa - luxury spa at Cascade Wellness Resort

Why it ranks third: Cascade Spa is the most comprehensive spa facility in the Lagos area. Located at Cascade Wellness Resort on the cliffs above Porto de Mos Beach, it offers a heated outdoor pool, sauna, jacuzzi, Turkish bath, and ice fountain. The view over the Atlantic is part of the experience.

What you get: Full spa facilities, massage treatments, hot stone therapy, beauty treatments, and wellness packages. The Turkish bath and sauna are the core recovery draws.

Who it is for: People who want a luxury spa experience rather than a fitness class. Good for deep rest days, post-injury recovery, or simply disconnecting from training for an afternoon.

Note: Some facilities may have temporary availability changes. Check directly before booking.

Visit Cascade Spa

4. Le Dome by Claire Stable - therapeutic massage for surfers

Why it ranks fourth: Claire Stable is a certified Shiatsu therapist, foot reflexologist, and yoga teacher who has worked in Lagos since 2018. Her practice is built around the needs of surfers and active people. She understands the specific tension patterns that come from paddling, popping up, and long hours in the water.

What you get: Shiatsu massage, foot reflexology, and therapeutic bodywork tailored to athletes. Sessions focus on shoulder mobility, lower back release, and hip alignment.

Who it is for: Surfers, runners, and anyone with repetitive strain or chronic tension. If you have a specific injury or tightness pattern, this is targeted work rather than general relaxation.

Visit Le Dome by Claire Stable

5. InLight Studio - established yoga and breathwork

Why it ranks fifth: InLight is the longest-running yoga studio in Lagos old town. It offers the most consistent daily schedule, which matters when you are building a routine rather than dropping in for a single class.

What you get: Vinyasa, Hatha, Rocket Yoga, Ashtanga, Yin. The full spectrum from gentle to demanding.

Who it is for: Students who want structure and variety. Good for travellers who want to attend daily classes for a week or more, and for residents who want a regular practice.

Visit InLight Studio

6. Espiche Yoga - yoga, pilates, aerial and breathwork

Why it ranks sixth: Espiche Yoga, located just outside Lagos in the village of Espiche, offers more styles than any other studio in the area. Aerial yoga, in particular, is hard to find elsewhere in the western Algarve.

What you get: Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Aerial yoga, pilates, and breathwork workshops. The space is in a converted building with high ceilings.

Who it is for: Variety seekers and people who want to try aerial yoga. Worth the short trip from Lagos for the aerial classes alone.

7. The Surf Experience - surf camp with yoga and pilates

Why it ranks seventh: The Surf Experience, founded in 1995, is one of the original surf camps in the Algarve. Their packages include optional yoga and pilates sessions alongside surf training. For people who want surf plus recovery in one booking, this is the established option.

What you get: Surf lessons, surf guiding, yoga, pilates, SUP yoga, and kayak tours. Accommodation packages available.

Who it is for: Surfers who want an all-in-one active holiday. The yoga and pilates are designed specifically to complement surf training.

Visit The Surf Experience

8. Soluna Space Lagos - workshops and holistic therapies

Why it ranks eighth: Soluna is a wellness and events platform with two studios that host rotating teachers, workshops, and holistic therapies. The schedule changes week to week, which means less consistency but more variety.

What you get: Rotating yoga, movement classes, workshops, and therapies. Check the schedule before you go.

Who it is for: People who want workshops and one-off events rather than a regular class schedule.

9. In Harmony Centre - integrative health near the marina

Why it ranks ninth: In Harmony is a full integrative health centre near Lagos Marina. It offers pregnancy yoga, regular classes, and personal development workshops alongside other health services.

What you get: Yoga including pregnancy yoga, health therapies, and workshops.

Who it is for: People who want health services alongside yoga. Good for prenatal students and those interested in integrative wellness.

Price: Drop-in yoga classes 15 EUR.

10. Local massage therapists and independent practitioners

Why it ranks tenth: Lagos has a community of independent massage therapists and holistic practitioners who work from home studios or rent space by appointment. These are harder to find online but often deliver the most personalised care.

What you get: Deep tissue massage, sports massage, Thai massage, and holistic therapies. Quality varies, so ask locals for recommendations.

Who it is for: People who want one-on-one treatment without the studio environment. Often more flexible on timing and more affordable than resort spas.

How to find them: Ask at surf camps, yoga studios, or local Facebook groups. Word of mouth is the best filter in Lagos.

Recovery for surfers: how to sequence your day

If you surf in the morning, your body needs three things: heat to relax tight muscles, movement to restore range of motion, and stillness to let your nervous system settle. The ideal Lagos recovery day looks like this:

Morning: Surf session.

Midday: Light meal and hydration.

Afternoon: Infrared sauna at Algarve Alchemy or a Heat Recovery class at Heat Lagos.

Evening: Yin yoga or massage.

That sequence, repeated across a surf trip, is the difference between waking up stiff and waking up ready for the next session.

For travellers: building a wellness itinerary

If you are visiting Lagos for a week, book at least two different experiences. A heated class at Heat Lagos plus a contrast session at Algarve Alchemy covers movement and recovery. Add a massage from Claire Stable if you have surf-related tension. If you want pure rest, spend an afternoon at Cascade Spa.

Most studios offer drop-in pricing between 12 and 22 EUR. Heat Lagos offers a 7-day Vacation Week pass for 59 EUR, which covers all classes. Cascade Spa treatments require advance booking, especially in peak season.
