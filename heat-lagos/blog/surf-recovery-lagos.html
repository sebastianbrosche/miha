Surf Recovery

10 Best Surf Recovery Classes in Lagos, Portugal (2026)

Surfing breaks your body down. These are the best places in Lagos to build it back up. Yoga, pilates, massage, and contrast therapy.

Why surfers need recovery

Surfing is repetitive and asymmetrical. You paddle for hours with your shoulders in the same position. You pop up explosively, twisting your lower back. You hold tension in your neck and hips. Then you sit in cold water, which tightens everything further.

The result: tight shoulders, stiff lower back, restricted hip mobility, and a nervous system that stays in fight-or-flight mode long after you leave the water. Without recovery, those patterns compound. With the right recovery, you surf longer, perform better, and avoid injury.

Professional surfers know this. Kelly Slater and Stephanie Gilmore both use pilates as part of their training. The reason is simple: pilates builds the core stability, spinal alignment, and hip mobility that surfing demands. Yoga adds flexibility and breath control. Massage releases the tension that builds up from repetition. Heat therapy relaxes muscles faster than rest alone.

The recovery toolkit: what actually works

Before the list, here is what each type of recovery does and when to use it:

Infrared heat: Warms muscles from the inside, increasing blood flow and making tissues more pliable. Best used within a few hours of surfing, before stretching.

Yin yoga: Long holds in supported positions that release fascia and restore range of motion. Best in the evening, after the body has warmed up.

Pilates: Core strength, spinal alignment, and hip stability. Best on rest days or as a morning practice before surfing.

Sauna and ice bath: Contrast therapy that reduces inflammation and trains the nervous system. Best on heavy surf days or after long sessions.

Massage: Releases chronic tension and restores shoulder and hip mobility. Best when you have specific tightness or after multi-day surf trips.

Why it ranks first: Heat Lagos is the only studio in Lagos that offers recovery classes in an infrared-heated room. That matters because infrared heat penetrates deeper than forced air or hot towels, warming muscles from the inside and making them more receptive to release work. The space is also the most modern in town - plenty of room, new equipment, and a fitness-focused approach that prioritises results over spirituality.

What makes it different: Most yoga studios in Lagos focus on spiritual, gentle flows. Most pilates studios focus on slow reformer work. Heat Lagos offers modern, challenging classes with real physical results. When you want to wind down and recover, Heat Yin is very popular - long holds in the warmth that release fascia and calm the nervous system. For active recovery, Heat Recovery and Heat Mobility target the specific areas that surfers need - shoulders, hips, and lower back.

Classes for surfers:

  • Heat Recovery: Gentle mobility in the warmth, targeting shoulders, hips, and lower back. Designed for active bodies that need release without aggressive stretching.
  • Heat Yin: Long still holds in the warmth to release fascia and calm the nervous system. Very popular after a heavy surf day.
  • Heat Mobility: Targeted joint and muscle work for people who train regularly. Good for maintaining range of motion between surf sessions.

Who it is for: Surfers who want recovery that actually works. The infrared heat makes the difference - you get more from every minute of the class than you would in a normal-temperature room.

2. Algarve Alchemy - sauna, ice bath and somatic recovery

Why it ranks second: Algarve Alchemy is the only place in Lagos old town where you can do a full contrast therapy session - infrared sauna followed by ice bath - without needing a gym membership or hotel booking. The space is designed for it.

What you get: Contrast journeys (sauna plus ice bath), somatic movement classes, and yoga. The combination of heat, cold, and gentle movement is one of the most effective recovery protocols for surfers.

Who it is for: Surfers who want the full recovery experience. The ice bath, in particular, reduces inflammation in shoulders and lower back faster than passive rest.

3. Le Dome by Claire Stable - therapeutic massage for surfers

Why it ranks third: Claire Stable has worked in Lagos since 2018 and specialises in the tension patterns that surfers develop. She is a certified Shiatsu therapist and foot reflexologist who understands what paddling, popping up, and long hours in the water do to the body.

What you get: Shiatsu massage, foot reflexology, and therapeutic bodywork. Sessions target the shoulders, lower back, and hips - the three areas that suffer most in surfing.

Who it is for: Surfers with chronic tension, recurring tightness, or specific pain patterns. If you have been surfing for multiple days in a row, this is the most targeted recovery option in Lagos.

4. InLight Studio - Vinyasa and Yin for shoulder mobility

Why it ranks fourth: InLight is the most established yoga studio in Lagos, with the widest daily schedule. For surfers, the Vinyasa classes build shoulder mobility and the Yin classes release the hips and lower back. The consistency matters - you can attend daily for a week without repeating the same class.

Classes for surfers: Vinyasa (shoulder mobility and breath), Yin (hip and back release), Hatha (foundational strength).

Who it is for: Surfers who want traditional yoga without heat. Good for building a daily routine during a longer stay.

Price: Drop-in around 15-18 EUR.

5. The Surf Experience - surf camp with yoga and pilates included

Why it ranks fifth: The Surf Experience, founded in 1995, is one of the original surf camps in the Algarve. Their packages include optional yoga and pilates sessions designed specifically to complement surf training. For surfers who want everything organised in one booking, this is the established option.

What you get: Surf lessons, surf guiding, yoga, pilates, SUP yoga, and kayak tours. Accommodation packages available.

Who it is for: Surfers visiting Lagos who want an all-in-one package. The yoga and pilates are built into the schedule, so you do not have to think about recovery - it is automatic.

Visit The Surf Experience

6. Espiche Yoga - pilates and breathwork for core stability

Why it ranks sixth: Espiche Yoga, located just outside Lagos in Espiche, offers pilates and breathwork alongside their yoga schedule. The pilates classes focus on core strength and spinal alignment, which directly supports surf performance.

What you get: Pilates, Hatha, Vinyasa, breathwork workshops. The breathwork is particularly useful for surfers who want to improve lung capacity and calm their nervous system before big sessions.

Who it is for: Surfers who want pilates and breathwork specifically. Worth the short trip from Lagos.

7. The Movement Lab - pilates with a dance perspective

Why it ranks seventh: The Movement Lab approaches pilates from a dancer's perspective, which means more focus on hip mobility, spinal articulation, and controlled movement. These are the exact qualities surfers need for fluid turns and stable pop-ups.

What you get: Pilates, yoga, contemporary dance, urban dance. The cross-training opportunity is unique in Lagos.

Who it is for: Surfers who want to improve body awareness and movement quality, not just stretch tight muscles.

Price: Drop-in from 12 EUR.

8. Cascade Spa - luxury recovery at Cascade Wellness Resort

Why it ranks eighth: Cascade Spa is the most comprehensive spa facility in the Lagos area. The heated outdoor pool, sauna, jacuzzi, and Turkish bath give surfers a full recovery day without the intensity of a class. The cliff-top location over Porto de Mos Beach is part of the experience.

What you get: Spa facilities, massage treatments, hot stone therapy, and wellness packages.

Who it is for: Surfers who want passive recovery rather than active classes. Good for deep rest days or when you are too tired for movement.

Visit Cascade Spa

9. In Harmony Centre - gentle yoga and integrative health

Why it ranks ninth: In Harmony Centre, near Lagos Marina, offers gentle yoga classes that work well for surfers who are recovering from injury or need a slower approach. The integrative health context means you can combine yoga with other therapies.

What you get: Gentle yoga, health therapies, and workshops.

Who it is for: Surfers recovering from injury or dealing with chronic pain who need a gentler entry point.

Price: Drop-in yoga classes 15 EUR.

10. Self-directed recovery - the free options

Why it ranks tenth: Not every recovery session needs a studio. Lagos has natural recovery tools that cost nothing.

Beach walking: Walking on sand activates the small stabiliser muscles in your feet and ankles, which improves balance for surfing. Do it barefoot at low tide.

Ocean swimming: A slow swim after surfing helps flush lactic acid and restores shoulder range of motion. Keep it easy - no sprinting.

Cliff path walking: The coastal paths between Lagos and Luz are undulating and uneven, which activates the hips and core in ways that flat walking does not.

Cold ocean immersion: If you do not have access to an ice bath, a few minutes of passive floating in the ocean after your surf session has similar anti-inflammatory effects.

The ideal surf recovery week in Lagos

Here is a practical recovery schedule for a surfer visiting Lagos for a week:

Day 1 (arrival): Light surf session. Evening Heat Yin at Heat Lagos to release travel tension.

Day 2: Morning surf. Afternoon Heat Recovery at Heat Lagos for shoulders and hips.

Day 3: Morning surf. Afternoon contrast therapy at Algarve Alchemy (sauna plus ice bath).

Day 4 (rest day): No surf. Pilates at Espiche Yoga or The Movement Lab for core and alignment. Beach walk at sunset.

Day 5: Morning surf. Massage from Claire Stable for any specific tightness.

Day 6: Morning surf. Vinyasa at InLight for shoulder mobility.

Day 7 (departure): Light surf or rest. Heat Yin at Heat Lagos before travel.

For travellers: drop-in and package options

Most studios in Lagos welcome drop-ins. Heat Lagos offers a 7-day Vacation Week pass for 59 EUR, which covers all recovery, yoga, pilates, and sculpt classes. Algarve Alchemy offers single contrast sessions. Claire Stable works by appointment. InLight and Espiche Yoga both offer single-class pricing.

If you want everything in one package, The Surf Experience includes yoga and pilates in their surf camp bookings.
