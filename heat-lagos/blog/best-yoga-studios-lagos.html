Lagos Guide

10 Best Yoga Studios in Lagos, Portugal (2026)

A curated guide to every yoga studio in Lagos. No pay-to-play, no sponsorships — just what each studio does best and who it suits.

How this list was made

This list is based on class variety, teacher quality, studio environment, and what makes each studio different from the others. We visited every studio, took classes, and spoke to teachers and students. We do not charge for placement and we do not accept sponsorships. If a studio is on this list, it earned its spot.

Lagos is small. Most studios are within a 4-minute walk of each other in or near the old town. All teach in English. Prices range from €12 to €22 for a single drop-in. Every studio on this list welcomes visitors and short-term residents.

Heat Lagos — infrared-heated yoga, pilates & recovery

What it does best: Heat Lagos is the most modern and spacious yoga studio in Lagos. The space is purpose-built with plenty of room to move (15-20 people), brand new equipment, and the only heated classes in town. The heat comes from infrared panels that warm the body directly, not forced air that dries out your throat. Classes run at around 30°C, warm enough to deepen the practice without the oppressive humidity of traditional hot yoga.

What makes it different: Darker, hotter, fresh and chill vibes with a focus on results. Very experienced instructors. Most yoga studios in Lagos focus on spiritual, traditional styles — chanting, meditation-heavy sessions, and gentle flows. Very few offer genuinely challenging, modern fitness-focused classes. Heat Lagos is the exception. Every class is built around real physical results, not just relaxation.

Class types:

  • Heat Sculpt: The most popular class. A tough fitness session that combines pilates, strength training, and high reps in the infrared heat. You will feel it for two days after. This is where you go when you want immediate results and a serious sweat.
  • Heat Power: Demanding heated yoga with strength elements.
  • Heat Flow: A more mellow heated Vinyasa. Good for when you want movement without intensity.
  • Heat Yin: Long still holds in the warmth. Very popular for winding down after a heavy training day or a long surf session.
  • Heat Pilates: A more precise and targeted class for learning technique and building supporting strength for your other life activities and hobbies / sports.
  • Heat Mobility and Heat Recovery: For active bodies that need release and range of motion.

Who it is for: Active people — surfers, runners, cyclists, padel players — who want to sweat, build strength and mobility, and recover properly. Also excellent for beginners because the heat makes muscles more pliable and the teachers offer options for every level.

Price: Intro Offer €79 for 2 weeks unlimited. Drop-in €22. Vacation Week €59 for 7 days. Monthly memberships from €125.

InLight Studio — the most established schedule in Lagos

What it does best: InLight is the longest-running yoga studio in Lagos old town. It offers the widest daily schedule, with classes from early morning through evening, seven days a week. The space is bright, clean, and purpose-built for yoga.

Class types: Vinyasa, Hatha, Rocket Yoga, Ashtanga, Yin. Classes range from gentle to demanding. Teachers are experienced and cue clearly in English.

Who it is for: Students who want a traditional yoga studio with consistent scheduling. Good for beginners who want structure and for experienced students who want Rocket or Ashtanga.

Price: Drop-in around €15-€18. Class passes available.

Algarve Alchemy — yoga, pilates, sauna & ice bath

What it does best: Alchemy is not just a yoga studio. It is a wellness destination. The space above Black & White Cafe in the old town combines yoga and pilates with contrast therapy — infrared sauna and ice bath. That makes it ideal for people who want recovery as part of their routine, not just exercise.

Class types: Yoga, pilates, somatic movement, special events, contrast journeys.

Who it is for: People who want wellness as a full experience. If you are visiting Lagos for a week and want one place that covers movement, heat, and cold, this is it.

Visit Algarve Alchemy

Espiche Yoga — the widest variety of styles

What it does best: Espiche Yoga, located just outside Lagos in the village of Espiche, offers more styles than any other studio in the area. Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Aerial yoga, pilates, and breathwork. The space is in a converted building with high ceilings and good natural light.

Class types: Hatha, Vinyasa, Ashtanga, Aerial yoga, pilates, breathwork workshops.

Who it is for: Students who want variety and are willing to travel a few minutes outside the old town. The aerial yoga classes are particularly popular and hard to find elsewhere in the Algarve.

Price: Drop-in from €15. Aerial classes may be priced separately.

Le Dome — bilingual yoga and pilates (English & French)

What it does best: Le Dome offers something no other studio in Lagos does: classes in English and French. The French community in the Algarve is large, and Le Dome serves it directly. The teaching quality is solid, the space is intimate, and the community is welcoming.

Class types: Vinyasa, Yin, Hatha yoga, pilates.

Who it is for: French speakers and bilingual students. Also good for travellers who want a smaller, more personal studio experience.

Price: Drop-in €14. Multiple class passes available.

In Harmony Centre — integrative health near the marina

What it does best: In Harmony is a full integrative health centre, not just a yoga studio. It offers pregnancy yoga, regular classes, and personal development workshops. The location near Lagos Marina makes it convenient for people staying in that area.

Class types: Yoga including pregnancy yoga, workshops.

Who it is for: Prenatal students, people interested in integrative wellness, and those staying near the marina.

Price: Drop-in yoga classes €15.

La Casa del Corpo — creative movement and aerial

What it does best: La Casa del Corpo brings a creative, artistic approach to movement. Aerial yoga, animal flow, pilates, and somatic dance. The space is beautiful and the teaching style is less traditional, more exploratory.

Class types: Aerial yoga, animal flow, pilates, somatic dance.

Who it is for: Students who want something different from standard Vinyasa. Creative movers, dancers, and people who get bored with repetitive formats.

The Movement Lab — yoga, pilates & contemporary dance

What it does best: The Movement Lab sits at the intersection of yoga, pilates, and dance. Contemporary and urban dance classes share the schedule with yoga, making it a natural fit for people who cross-train between disciplines.

Class types: Yoga, pilates, contemporary dance, urban dance.

Who it is for: Dancers and movers who want yoga and pilates as part of a broader movement practice.

Price: Drop-in classes from €12. 10-class cards available.

Soluna Space Lagos — rotating teachers and workshops

What it does best: Soluna is a wellness and events platform rather than a fixed yoga studio. Two beautiful studios host rotating teachers, workshops, and holistic therapies. The schedule changes week to week, which means variety but less consistency.

Class types: Rotating schedule of yoga, movement, workshops, therapies.

Who it is for: Students who want variety and are happy to check the schedule regularly. Excellent for workshops and one-off events.

Transform to Bliss — retreat-style yoga between Luz and Burgau

What it does best: Transform to Bliss is located between the villages of Luz and Burgau, about 15 minutes from Lagos old town. It offers a more retreat-style experience — yoga, breathwork, and Ayurveda in a quieter setting away from the town centre.

Class types: Yoga, breathwork, Ayurveda consultations.

Who it is for: People who want a slower, more immersive wellness experience. Best for those with transport, as it is outside Lagos.

For travellers: drop-in and short-term options

Every studio on this list welcomes drop-ins. Most offer single-class prices between €12 and €22. Heat Lagos offers a 7-day Vacation Week pass for €59 — unlimited classes for a full week — which is the best value if you are visiting for more than a couple of days.

Every studio here has its own character. Try a few and see what your body responds to.
