## On-Page SEO Requirements

### Every Page Needs:
1. **Title tag**: 50-60 chars, keyword first
2. **Meta description**: 150-160 chars, CTA included
3. **H1**: One per page, keyword included naturally
4. **H2-H3**: Semantic structure with related keywords
5. **Internal links**: 2-3 per page to other site pages
6. **Image alt text**: Descriptive, keyword where natural
7. **Schema markup**: Course schema for training pages

### Homepage Example:
- **Title**: Yoga Teacher Training Portugal | 200-Hour YTT Certification
- **H1**: 200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training in Portugal
- **Meta**: Join our Yoga Alliance certified 200-hour teacher training in Portugal. Intensive 3-week format. Small groups. Book your spot for 2025/2026.
